# Grill me

Interview me relentlessly about every part of this idea until we reach a shared understanding.

- Explore the codebase first if it exists, so your questions are specific.
- Ask ONE question at a time.
- For each question, give your recommended answer and a one-line reason.
- Walk down each branch of the design. Resolve dependencies one by one.
- Cover: who uses it, the main flows, edge cases, data, what is out of scope, and how we will know it works.
- If I answer "your call", take your recommendation and move on.
- Do not write a plan or code. Stop only when I say we are aligned, or when no real questions remain.
- At the end, list the key decisions in 5–15 short bullets, including what we decided NOT to do.
